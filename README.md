# customer_behavior_analysis
# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior using transactional data from **3,900 customer purchases** across multiple product categories.

The objective is to identify customer spending patterns, purchasing preferences, customer segments, discount usage, and subscription trends to generate meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow using Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze revenue and purchasing patterns across customer demographics.
- Identify high-spending customers who use discounts.
- Understand customer loyalty and repeat purchasing behavior.
- Evaluate subscription performance and customer engagement.
- Identify top-rated and best-selling products.
- Analyze shipping preferences and discount dependency.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

## 🔍 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Exploratory Data Analysis (Python)

- Loaded and explored the dataset using Pandas.
- Analyzed dataset structure and statistical summaries.
- Handled 37 missing values in the Review Rating column using category-wise median imputation.
- Standardized column names using snake_case.
- Created age_group and purchase_frequency_days features.
- Identified redundant columns and removed promo_code_used.
- Connected Python with PostgreSQL and transferred the cleaned dataset.

### 2. Business Analysis (Sql)

Performed SQL analysis to answer key business questions:

1. Revenue generated by male and female customers.
2. Identification of high-spending discount users.
3. Top 5 products based on average customer ratings.
4. Comparison of average purchase amounts by shipping type.
5. Spending and revenue comparison between subscribers and non-subscribers.
6. Products with the highest discount dependency.
7. Customer segmentation into New, Returning, and Loyal customers.
8. Top 3 most purchased products within each category.
9. Relationship between repeat purchases and subscription status.
10. Revenue contribution across different age groups.

### 3. Interactive Dashboard (Power BI)

Developed an interactive Customer Behavior Dashboard to visualize:

- Total customers
- Average purchase amount
- Average product rating
- Subscription distribution
- Revenue by product category
- Sales by category
- Revenue and sales by age group

The dashboard includes interactive filters for subscription status, gender, category, and shipping type.

## 📊 Key Business Insights

- **Customer Base:** Analyzed 3,900 customer transactions.
- **Customer Spending:** Average purchase amount was approximately $59.76.
- **Customer Ratings:** Average review rating was 3.75.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Identified 3,116 Loyal, 701 Returning, and 83 New customers.
- **Subscription Behavior:** 1,053 customers were subscribers, while 2,847 were non-subscribers.
- **Shipping Preferences:** Express shipping customers had a higher average purchase amount ($60.48) compared to Standard shipping ($58.46).
- **Discount Dependency:** Hats had the highest discount rate at 50%.
- **Revenue Analysis:** Male customers generated higher total revenue than female customers in this dataset.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

- **Boost Subscriptions:** Introduce exclusive benefits and incentives to encourage subscription adoption.
- **Improve Customer Loyalty:** Reward repeat buyers through loyalty programs.
- **Optimize Discount Strategies:** Review discount policies to balance sales growth and profit margins.
- **Improve Product Positioning:** Promote highly rated and best-selling products.
- **Targeted Marketing:** Focus campaigns on high-revenue customer segments and relevant product categories.

It highlights the ability to transform raw transactional data into meaningful insights and translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.
